# Code of conduct

Keep participation respectful and focused on the project. Welcome newcomers, give specific constructive feedback, and respect privacy. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and sharing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to keep discussions productive. Report conduct concerns through a private channel offered by the maintainers. If none is available, request one without posting personal or sensitive details publicly.
